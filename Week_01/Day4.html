About Sindh High Court

The Sindh High Court (SHC) is the highest court in the province of Sindh, Pakistan. It is located in Karachi and plays an important role in the administration of justice throughout the province. The Court deals with constitutional, civil, criminal, and other important legal matters. It also supervises the subordinate courts working under its jurisdiction.

Role of the Court

The Sindh High Court performs several important functions:

  • Protects the fundamental rights of citizens.
  • Hears appeals and revisions from lower courts.
  • Deals with constitutional petitions.
  • Supervises subordinate courts in Sindh.
  • Interprets and applies the Constitution and laws of Pakistan.
  • Provides legal remedies to people when their rights are violated.

Quick Facts
